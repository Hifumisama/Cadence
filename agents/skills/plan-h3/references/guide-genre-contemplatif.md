# Genre "contemplatif" — letting a place or a state breathe

1. **Few, long shots**: a single held shot, or two. One shot every 3–4 s at the fastest.
2. **Locked camera or very slow movement**, and say so: a background that does not move is half the effect.
3. **Movement is discreet and continuous**: wind in a material, water, clouds, sliding light, breath. Describe what moves **gently** and what stays still.
4. **Add no action to fill the time.** If nothing happens, it is intended: give the plan an intention (a wait, a grandeur) rather than events.
5. **Sound carries the space**: a rich, precise ambience; sparse or absent music (`N/A`).
6. **No push-in or dolly** toward the subject: held camera, then a clean pan if needed.
